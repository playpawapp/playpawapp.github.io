Playpaw

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

The short version
  • Your cat's name, your schedule and your play history stay on your iPhone.
  • Cat photos are checked on your device and are never uploaded to our servers.
  • We can't see which apps you use or block. Apple's Screen Time keeps that private, even from us.
  • We don't track you and there are no ads or advertising SDKs in the app.
  • We never sell your data.

Who we are

Playpaw ("we", "us") is an iOS app that locks distracting apps at playtime until you've spent time playing with your cat. This policy explains what information the app handles and why. If you have questions, email us at playpawapp@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

To work, Playpaw saves the following on your iPhone only:

  • Your cat's name.
  • Your playtime schedule, session length and lock settings.
  • Your answers during setup, such as hours per day on your phone and how you heard about us.
  • Your play history: completed sessions, streaks, paws and items in your cat's room.
  • Photos you take of your cat to finish a session.

We don't have access to this information. Deleting the app deletes it.

Cat photos

When you finish a session, you take a photo of your cat. Playpaw uses Apple's on-device image recognition to check that a cat is in the picture. The photo is processed on your iPhone and is not sent to us or to anyone else. Playpaw asks for camera access only for this.

Screen Time and blocked apps

Playpaw uses Apple's Screen Time framework (Family Controls) to lock the apps you choose. Apple gives Playpaw anonymous tokens instead of app names, so we can't see which apps you selected or how you use them. This information never leaves your device and is used only to lock and unlock apps on your schedule.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Playpaw sends playtime reminders and a reminder before your free trial ends. They are scheduled on your device. You can turn them off anytime in iPhone Settings.

Subscriptions and payments

Purchases are handled by Apple through your Apple Account. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app whether your subscription is active so we can unlock premium features.

No tracking or ads

Playpaw doesn't track you across apps or websites, doesn't use the advertising identifier and contains no ads or advertising SDKs.

Crash and usage data

If you've agreed to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with us. We use them only to fix bugs.

Data we don't collect

We don't ask you to create an account. We don't collect your name, email, contacts, location or browsing history, and we don't sell personal data.

Children

Playpaw is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices and rights

  • You can turn off notifications, camera and Screen Time access in iPhone Settings at any time.
  • You can delete all data stored by Playpaw by deleting the app.
  • Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data. Email us and we'll help within 30 days. In the EU you can also complain to your local data protection authority; in Poland that's the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

Changes to this policy

If we add features that handle data differently, for example sharing your streak with a partner, we'll update this page and the date above before the feature ships.
